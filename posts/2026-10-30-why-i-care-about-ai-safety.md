## A question I've been thinking about

As language models become increasingly capable,
I've become interested in understanding not just
what they produce, but what happens internally
before they produce an answer.

## Why this interests me

One question I keep returning to is whether
we can detect internal signals associated
with unreliable or unsafe model behaviour.

## What I want to explore next

I'm especially interested in model introspection,
mechanistic interpretability, and whether internal
representations can help us monitor and control
model behaviour.
