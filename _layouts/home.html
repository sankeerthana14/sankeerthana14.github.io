THE THROUGHLINE / 03 ✳

Reliability is more than
getting an answer right.

Across different domains, I keep returning to the same idea: can we recognise when a system is headed toward an unreliable decision—and give it a better action to take?

01 / Observe

Find the evidence and model signals that matter.

02 / Understand

Ask what those signals reveal—and what they do not.

03 / Intervene

Retrieve, verify, abstain, or steer more carefully.
